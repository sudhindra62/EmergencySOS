# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

## PROJECT: ROADGUARDIAN AI

### Tagline: "Agentic AI Golden Hour Emergency Response Platform"

---

## 1. VISION & OBJECTIVE

To establish the world’s first autonomous **Agentic AI Emergency Operating System (EOS)** designed to optimize response times during the critical "Golden Hour" of national highway accidents. By substituting reactive, fragmented telephone triage systems with multi-agent consensus networks, RoadGuardian AI reduces coordination delays from up to 45 minutes to **under 12 minutes**, actively preserving human life.

---

## 2. THE PROBLEM STATEMENT

India’s national highway network suffers from delayed emergency management due to:

- **Caller Duress:** Panicked bystanders struggle to describe physical landmarks, verify passenger vitals or triage trauma severity under severe shock.
- **Fragmented Dispatch (Siloed Ops):** Police traffic networks, GVK EMRI 108 medical responders, and public/private hospitals do not share data. Ambulances lose precious time drifting between filled emergency rooms.
- **The Bystander Guidance Gap:** Bystanders who want to assist lack basic first-aid training. Unconscious passengers are pulled out dangerously, exacerbating cervical spine traumas and causing irreversible damage.

---

## 3. CORE USER PERSONAS

1.  **Aditya (Bystander/Highway Traveler):** Needs hands-free, clear, zero-jargon guidance to provide immediate live-saving support like direct pressure on scale wounds.
2.  **Paramedic Sneha (Trauma Responder):** Needs advanced digital alerts containing passenger GCS scores, vehicle entrapment markers, and specialized hospital bed bookings en-route.
3.  **Major Gen. Roy (MoRTH Inspector):** Needs macro district-level data visualizations matching highway accident black spots to prioritize structural signaling upgrades.

---

## 4. SYSTEM CHARACTERISTICS & MAJOR CAPABILITIES

To ensure high-fidelity resilience under emergency scenarios, the platform implements:

- **Dark Glassmorphic Command interface:** Clean, high legibility type treatment designed for high-stress dispatch rooms operating under low-light configurations.
- **Server-Side Gemini AI Assessment:** Utilizes the Google GenAI SDK to evaluate written reports, transcribed voices, or images to assign severity tiers (Minor, Moderate, Severe, Critical).
- **10-Agent Synchronous Consensus:** Orchestrates a network where specialist agencies (Ambulance dispatch, Highway police, Trauma Discovery, Offline Survival) deliberate concurrently to generate unified emergency dispatches.
- **Zero-Internet Offline Survival:** Local browser-cached first aid directories and high-compression SMS telegram packagers (`RG_SOS#C|V2|H1|L13.04,80.15`) that fit within a 160-character cellular block, enabling transit alerts from cellular deadzones.

---

## 5. REVENUE STUDY & COMPETITIVE EDGE

- **Zero-Barrier Onboarding:** No logins required for immediate caller reporting. Integrated GPS triggers instantly.
- **Government-Ready Bridge:** Adheres to MoRTH accident classification models and integrates with public 112 CAD platforms.
- **Intellectual IP:** Leverages structural NLP models to prioritize advanced trauma units (ALS vs BLS) ahead of ambulance arrival.
